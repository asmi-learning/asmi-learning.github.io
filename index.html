Comprehensive Curriculum Architecture

Curriculum Coverage & Syllabus Map

Designed to cover and exceed the rigorous Linear Algebra syllabi for undergraduates, postgraduates, and AI/engineering researchers.

Unit I

Systems of Linear Equations

Matrices and elementary row operations, row-reduced echelon form (RREF), rank of a matrix, solution of linear systems via Gauss elimination and LU decomposition (matrix factorization), matrix multiplication and invertible matrices.

Unit II

Vector Spaces & Subspaces

Axiomatic vector spaces and properties, subspaces, linear dependence and independence, linear combinations and span, basis and dimension, sum and intersection of subspaces, direct sums, dual spaces, invariant and cyclic subspaces.

Unit III

Linear Transformations

Linear maps and operational properties, range and kernel, rank and nullity of transformations, Rank-Nullity Theorem and proof consequences, matrix representations of operators, change of basis, isomorphism theorems, and change of coordinate matrices.

Unit IV

Inner Product Spaces

Scalar inner products, orthogonality, normed linear spaces, complex inner products, orthogonal complements, subspace projections, Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization process, least squares approximation, and Bessel's inequality.

Unit V

Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors

Eigenvalues and eigenvectors of operators and matrices, eigenspaces, algebraic and geometric multiplicity, Cayley-Hamilton Theorem, similarity and diagonalization, symmetric and Hermitian matrices, orthogonal diagonalization, quadratic forms, and conic sections.

Unit VI

Advanced Applications & SVD

Spectral decomposition, Singular Value Decomposition (SVD), dimensionality reduction, Principal Component Analysis (PCA), algorithmic image compression, facial recognition (eigenfaces), and solving decoupled systems of first-order linear ODEs.
