Privacy Policy

Compliance: GDPR (EU & UK), DPDP (India) • Version 1.0

1. Commitment to Minimal Data Collection

ASMI Learning operates under a strict principle of data minimization. We do not maintain marketing mailing lists, we do not deploy commercial tracking cookies, and we do not sell or monetize personal information.

2. Data We Collect

When you request a syllabus audit or mentorship consultation, we collect solely:

  • Your Name
  • Your Email Address
  • Your University / Academic Institution
  • Your Course Outline or Mathematical Problem Topics

This information is collected exclusively to evaluate your academic profile and coordinate mentorship sessions.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Trackers

This website is hosted statically via GitHub Pages (Fastly CDN). We do not use Google AdSense, Meta Pixel, or third-party behavioral trackers. Technical server connection logs are maintained solely for DDoS prevention and global delivery security.

4. Your Rights (GDPR Articles 15–20)

Regardless of whether you are located in the UK, European Union, US, or globally, you retain the right to:

  • Request a copy of any communication records stored between you and ASMI.
  • Request the immediate and permanent deletion of your email and contact records from our inbox (Right to Erasure).
