Privacy Policy

Compliance: GDPR (EU & UK), DPDP (India) • Version 1.1

1. Commitment to Minimal Data Collection

ASMI Learning (operated by Dr. Dhabalendu Samanta, Independent Educational Consultant & Data Controller) adheres to strict data minimization. We do not sell personal data, maintain automated tracking cookies, or deploy marketing trackers.

2. Data We Collect

When you request a syllabus audit or mentorship consultation via email, we receive solely:

  • Your Name
  • Your Email Address
  • Your University / Academic Institution
  • Your Course Syllabus or Mathematical Roadblock Notes

This information is used exclusively to evaluate your academic profile and coordinate mentorship sessions.

3. Zero Third-Party Tracking

This website is hosted statically via GitHub Pages. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, or commercial advertising scripts. Server connection logs are maintained solely for cybersecurity and DDoS mitigation.

4. Your Rights (GDPR Articles 15–20)

Regardless of whether you are located in the UK, European Union, US, or globally, you retain the right to request:

  • A summary of any communication records stored between you and ASMI Learning.
  • The immediate and permanent deletion of your email and contact records from our inbox (Right to Erasure).

5. Data Controller Contact

For any privacy requests or data deletion demands, contact Dr. Dhabalendu Samanta directly at dhabalendu@gmail.com.
